---
title: Live preview
description: Let the admin frame your frontend and render each unsaved edit in it, with connectPreview from @contentworker/sdk/live-preview.
---

Live preview shows an editor their edits on your real site before they save. The admin frames
your frontend; your page connects to it, receives the editor's unsaved values for the entries it
shows, and re-renders. The admin draws selection and hover outlines itself, outside the frame,
from the boxes your page reports. Nothing reaches the API from your page: saving is the editor's
ordinary save.

## In the editor

Give the content type a [preview URL](/guides/content-modeling/#preview-url), and the entry
editor's side pane gains a **Site** view beside Details and Preview: the entry's page on your site,
framed, re-rendered as the editor types. Saving works exactly as it does without it. A type with
no preview URL has no Site view.

- **Edit** and **View**: in edit mode a click on a marked element focuses that field (or that
  block, in its blocks field) in the form, and the field focused in the form is outlined on the
  page and scrolled into view. In view mode your page behaves as it does on its own.
- **Desktop**, **Tablet** and **Phone** render your page at 1280, 768 or 390 pixels wide, scaled
  down to fit the pane, so a layout never depends on how wide the editor's window is.
- A person whose role can't edit the entry gets clicks only: the admin accepts `select` (and
  `scroll`, `layout`) and never sends their unsaved values.

The admin frames your preview URL as it is, filled for the entry with a preview token that lasts
an hour; your page connects to it. Your page must allow the admin to frame it: if it sends
`Content-Security-Policy: frame-ancestors` or `X-Frame-Options`, name the admin's origin. The
admin frames it sandboxed (`allow-scripts allow-same-origin allow-forms allow-popups`: no
navigating the editor's window, no downloads) and sends no `Referer`. A link your page follows
stays connected if it goes to an address one of the space's preview URLs names; anywhere else,
the pane says the page moved away and offers the way back.

If your page never offers to connect, the pane says it isn't connected and shows it as saved. If
its SDK shares no protocol version with the admin, the pane says whether it is too old or too
new. If it doesn't report the entry in `page`, the pane says the page doesn't show it.

## The canvas

A type with a preview URL and a [blocks field](/reference/field-types/#blocks) also opens on the
**canvas**: switch the entry editor from **Form** to **Canvas** (`/content/<type>/<entry>/canvas`).
The canvas is your page, framed nearly the full width of the admin, with the editor's tools drawn
over it. It edits the same draft as the form.

- **Seams.** Each slot your page marks with `data-cw-slot` (a blocks field, or a slot inside a
  block, see [the `layout` extension](#extensions)) shows a dashed seam between its blocks, named
  with the slot and how full it is ("Main · 3 of 8", or "Features · 0 of 2–4" when the slot sets
  `min` and `max`). Choose a seam, or select a block and press <kbd>/</kbd>: the seam is marked
  with a solid line, and the **dock** opens along the bottom as one row of cards (← and → move
  across them) with exactly the blocks that slot takes: its field's block types, which a
  [block catalog](/guides/blocks-from-code/) push writes from each slot's `allowedChildren`, under
  the catalog's groups with a line of each block's description. Your page is scrolled so the seam
  stays above the dock. A full slot says so and offers nothing. Thumbnails aren't drawn; each
  block is its name.
- **Inserting** puts the block in your page at once, unsaved, and selects it.
- **The inspector** edits the selected block: **Content** (its own fields, with the form's
  editors), **Data** (references to entries and assets) and **Look** (props limited to listed
  values, as segmented controls or a select; never free CSS). Move, duplicate and remove are
  there too. A block's slots are filled from the page's seams, or from **Add to …** here.
- **Layers** opens on demand: every block as an outline, slot by slot. It is also the full
  keyboard and screen-reader path to every block (the page itself isn't navigable from the
  admin): ↑ and ↓ move through the page, ← to the block holding this one, → into it, Enter opens
  its settings, and each top-level slot has its own Add.
- **Keys:** ⌘↑ and ⌘↓ move a block in its slot, Delete removes it (with Undo in the toast), ⌘Z and
  ⌘⇧Z undo and redo, ⌘S saves, Esc closes the dock or clears the selection. They are in the
  shortcuts list (<kbd>?</kbd>).
- **Device** widths render your page at 1280, 768 or 390 pixels wide, scaled to fit, as in the
  Site view.

Every change is a block patch (the same key-addressed operations as
[`PATCH …/entries/:id/blocks`](/reference/rest-api/)), applied in the admin and sent to your
page as `blocks.patch`; undo applies its inverse. **Save draft** is the entry's ordinary save, held
to the version the canvas started from: if someone saved the entry meanwhile, the canvas says so and
lets you load their version or save yours over it (theirs stays in the version history). Before it
saves, the canvas lists what would stop the save: a required slot with too few blocks, a block
your code no longer provides ("Removed from code": keep it for now or remove it; the entry can't be
saved while it holds one), and any other field a block needs.

The canvas says in place why it isn't editing: the type has no preview URL (with the way back to
the form), the person's role can only read the entry, the entry is published and unchanged (edit a
draft to begin), or the screen is narrower than an iPad held upright, where it is for review. From
iPad width to a desktop's, the dock, the inspector and Layers open as sheets, by tap. A page whose
SDK doesn't report its layout gets no seams: blocks are still selected by a click in the page or in
Layers, and added from Layers. A page that never connects shows the same notices as the Site view.

## Connect

```ts
import { connectPreview } from '@contentworker/sdk/live-preview';

const preview = connectPreview({
  adminOrigin: 'https://your-workspace.contentworker.app',
  capabilities: ['select', 'patch.fields', 'patch.blocks', 'proposals', 'scroll'],
  async onState(entryId, locale, fields, view) {
    // Re-render the entry from `view.withProposals` (the unsaved values, plus any proposals).
    await render(entryId, view.withProposals, view.ghosts);
  },
});

preview.emit('page', {
  url: location.href,
  entries: [{ entryId: entry.id, contentType: entry.contentType, locale: 'en-US' }],
});
preview.on('select', (ref) => highlight(ref));
// On teardown:
preview.close();
```

`connectPreview` does nothing unless the page is in a frame and `adminOrigin` is set, so you can
call it on every page. `adminOrigin` must be the admin's exact origin (https; http only on
`localhost`) and must differ from your page's own origin. **Settings → Connect a frontend** prints
it, and checks that a page connects ([Connect a frontend](/guides/connect-a-frontend/)). Your page keeps its own scrolling in the
frame: sticky headers, full-height heroes and lazy loading behave as on the live site.

`emit('page', …)` tells the admin which entries the page renders, so it knows whose state to
send. Report the entry being edited and the entries it directly references. Keep one connection
per page; after a client-side navigation it fires `disconnect`, offers again, and sends the next
page you report to the new session. Tell it about such navigations with
`preview.emit('navigated', { url })`; it notices the back and forward buttons itself.

| Capability     | Lets the admin                                                              |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `select`       | Receive clicks and hovers on marked elements, outline one, turn editing on and off |
| `patch.fields` | Send unsaved field values                                                   |
| `patch.blocks` | Send key-addressed block operations                                          |
| `proposals`    | Send Coworker's proposed blocks, which you render as ghosts                  |
| `scroll`       | Ask the page to scroll a marked element into view                           |
| `layout`       | Receive where your blocks and slots are, to draw insertion points (an extension, below) |

Capabilities are fixed when the page connects. The admin may use fewer, never more; a message
that needs one you didn't offer is refused.

In React, `useLivePreview` from `@contentworker/sdk/react` does the same for as long as the
component is mounted:

```tsx
import { useLivePreview } from '@contentworker/sdk/react';

function Landing({ entry }) {
  const live = useLivePreview({
    adminOrigin: 'https://your-workspace.contentworker.app',
    page: { entries: [{ entryId: entry.id, contentType: entry.contentType, locale: 'en-US' }] },
  });
  const fields = live.entries[entry.id]?.view.withProposals ?? entry.fields;
  // …render from fields
}
```

It offers every capability unless you pass `capabilities`, and returns `{ active, connected,
entries, preview }`. It reports `page` to each new session and again whenever `page` changes, and
empties `entries` when a session ends. The admin hears an edit is on screen once React has
committed it.

## Render the state

`onState(entryId, locale, fields, view)` is called once the admin sends an entry's state, then
after each edit. `fields` is one locale's values, typed as the Management API types them: rich
text as a `RichTextDocument`, blocks as `{ _type, _key, … }`, references as links. Nothing arrives
as HTML or Markdown: render from the same structures you get from Delivery or Preview.

- `fields` is the editor's unsaved state: what saving would save.
- `view.withProposals` is `fields` with Coworker's proposals laid over it. Render this one.
- `view.ghosts` lists the proposals' blocks by `_key`: `inserted` (proposed new), `changed`
  (proposed edits) and `removed` (proposed for removal, still present in `withProposals`). Render
  those as ghosts, for example translucent with a dashed outline, so the editor can tell a
  proposal from a change. A proposal is never part of `fields`.

Edits that arrive while `onState` is still running are folded into the next call, so a slow
render never queues up a backlog. `onState` may return a promise: the admin hears the edit is on
screen when it settles. If it throws, the admin is told the render failed and sends the whole
state again; nothing the failed call held counts as rendered until a later call succeeds.

## Mark up your page

Mark every element that renders a field, or a block, with its content ref in `data-cw-ref` (the
same attribute [inline editing](/guides/sdks/#inline-editing) uses). Inside blocks, the ref's
field is a path with each block's `_key` in brackets:

| Element                    | Ref field                     |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| A top-level field          | `title`                       |
| A block's root element     | `body[0192f…]`                |
| A field inside a block     | `body[0192f…].heading`        |
| A field in a nested block  | `body[0192f…].ctas[0192e…].label` |

A key, not a position, names each block, so a ref still points at the same block after a
reorder. A key holding `[`, `]` or `%` is written escaped (`%5B`, `%5D`, `%25`), so any key
round-trips. `formatFieldPath` and `parseFieldPath` from `@contentworker/sdk` build and read paths,
and `refAttrs(ref)` from `@contentworker/sdk/live-preview` gives the attribute to spread. A ref
with a path opens in the admin's entry form on its top-level field.

A blocks field rendered with `<Blocks>` (React) or `<CwBlocks>` (Vue) is marked for you: pass it the
field's ref as `preview` and `view.ghosts` as `ghosts`, and every block, field and slot carries its
mark, and each ghost `data-cw-ghost` (see
[Rendering blocks](/guides/blocks-from-code/#in-a-live-preview)).

## Editing in the frame

While the admin has editing on, a click on a marked element selects it in the admin instead of
reaching your page's handlers, and hovering reports the element under the pointer at most once a
frame. Links outside marked elements work as usual: the page navigates, says where it went, and
connects again on the new page. With editing off, the page behaves exactly as on its own.

If the page also mounts the [inline editor](/guides/sdks/#inline-editing), the connection drives
it: its toggle is hidden, the admin turns editing on and off, and it draws nothing in the page.

## The channel

Every message is `{ v, channel, kind, … }`. The page makes a fresh random `channel` for each page
load and offers it, with the protocol versions it speaks and its capabilities, in
`connect.offer`; it resends the offer up to three times, a second apart. The admin answers
`connect.accept { version, capabilities }`, or `connect.reject { reason, versions }` when the two
share no version, in which case the page logs one console error naming both lists: update
`@contentworker/sdk`.

Each side drops a message unless it comes from the other side's origin, from the other side's
window, on the session's channel, at the session's version. A message that then fails its schema
is dropped whole and counted (`preview.dropped`), never partly applied. Kinds a side doesn't know
are ignored, so a newer admin can talk to an older page at their shared version.

| From admin       | Carries                                                       |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `connect.accept` | `version`, `capabilities`                                     |
| `connect.reject` | `reason`, `versions`                                          |
| `entry.state`    | `rev`, `entryId`, `locale`, `fields`: the whole unsaved state |
| `entry.patch`    | `rev`, `entryId`, `locale`, `fields`: changed values (`null` clears one) |
| `blocks.patch`   | `rev`, `entryId`, `locale`, `field`, `ops`: block operations, some `proposed: true` |
| `select`         | `ref`, or `null` to clear                                     |
| `scroll`         | `ref`, `align?` (`start`, `center`, `end`) and `inset?` (extension) |
| `editing`        | `on` (extension)                                              |

| From page       | Carries                                                       |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `connect.offer` | `versions`, `capabilities`, `channel`, `sdk { name, version }` |
| `page`          | `url`, `entries [{ entryId, contentType, locale }]`           |
| `navigated`     | `url`                                                         |
| `pointer`       | `pointer` (`hover` or `click`), `ref`, `rect`                  |
| `applied`       | `rev`, `entryId`                                              |
| `failed`        | `rev`, `reason`, `entryId`                                    |
| `layout`        | `blocks`, `slots`, `fields`, `scroll`, `viewport`, `docHeight`, `truncated?` (extension) |

`rev` counts up by one per entry. The page applies a patch only when its `rev` is one more than
the last it applied for that entry, and answers anything else with `failed` and reason `gap`; the
admin then sends a fresh `entry.state`, which sets the count. A patch that fails its schema (reason
`invalid`), whose block operations can't be placed (`conflict`), or whose render threw (`render`)
is answered the same way. `applied { rev }` means every rev up to it is on screen.

A `blocks.patch` uses the operations of the Management API's block patch (`insert`, `update`,
`move`, `remove`, `duplicate`), addressed by `_key`. An op with `proposed: true` is Coworker's
proposal: a proposed `insert` adds a ghost block, a proposed `update` or `move` shows a proposed
change, a proposed `remove` marks a block, and a proposed `remove` of a proposed block takes it
back, with everything proposed in or beside it. A proposal names every block it brings by
`_key`. A fresh `entry.state` clears every proposal; the admin sends them again. `proposals` is
offered only with `patch.blocks`.

Limits: a message is at most 256 KB (an `entry.state`, 2 MB); a `blocks.patch` has at most 100
ops; a page reports at most 50 entries (`PAGE_REPORT_LIMIT`), the first the site lists, so list
the edited entry first. The admin accepts up to 200 (`MAX_PAGE_ENTRIES`), as sites on
`@contentworker/sdk` 0.3.0 send.

## Extensions

Additions both sides may ignore, so they need no new protocol version:

- **`layout` capability.** The page reports, in `layout`, the box of every block, field and slot.
  Mark the element directly holding a slot's blocks with `data-cw-slot`, holding the slot's ref
  (`slotAttrs(ref)`; the path is `body`, or `body[0192f…].items` inside a block). Each slot lists
  its gaps: the place before its first block, between each pair and after its last, with the
  insertion `index` and the blocks' keys on either side. The report is sent when something moves,
  at most once a frame and only when it changed; a page with more boxes than one message holds
  sends those on screen first and marks the message `truncated`. At most 2,000 blocks, slots and
  fields each, and 4,000 gaps.
- **`editing`.** The admin turns in-page editing on and off. It is on when the page connects with
  `select`.
- **`disconnect`.** `preview.on('disconnect')` fires when a session ends on a client-side
  navigation.
- **`pointer` with no ref.** A hover with `ref` and `rect` `null` says the pointer left every
  marked element.
- **`entryId` on `applied` and `failed`**, since `rev` counts per entry; and the failure reasons
  `invalid`, `conflict` and `render` beside `gap`.

## Your page and its scripts

Unsaved edits are rendered into your page, so any script it runs (analytics, tag managers,
third-party widgets) can read them while the editor works. Keep scripts you wouldn't trust with
drafts off your preview deployment.

Styling never crosses as CSS: a token prop arrives as a token name, which your page resolves from
its own manifest. Before writing any value into a CSS custom property, check it with
`isSafeTokenValue` from `@contentworker/sdk/live-preview`, and drop it whole when it fails. It
refuses `url(`, `@import`, `expression(`, `javascript:`, `<`, `>`, `\`, quotes, `&`, `;`, braces and
comments, and any function but `var`, `calc`, `clamp`, `min`, `max`, `rgb`, `hsl` and `oklch`, in
any case. Write the value through `style.setProperty` or your framework's style object, never by
building a `style` attribute or `<style>` text by hand.
