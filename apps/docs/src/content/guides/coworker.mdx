Coworker is ContentWorker's assistant: a chat panel in the admin. Ask it about your content, how a feature works or what
is waiting to be published, or have it create, tag, publish or schedule entries. It answers from
live data through tools, and anything that changes something waits for your confirm.

It works as you. Every tool call runs with your live permissions, in the space and environment
the header has chosen, so it can never do what you couldn't do yourself or what an
[MCP client](/guides/mcp/) with your access couldn't.

## Opening it

- The **Coworker** button in the header, or <kbd>⌘J</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>J</kbd>)
  anywhere, which also closes it.
- Changing something from a page. See [Edits from a page](#edits-from-a-page).

On a wide screen the panel is a column beside the page, and it reopens on your next visit if you
left it open. On a narrow screen, such as a phone, it is a sheet between the header and the tab
bar; <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes the sheet.

Enter sends and Shift+Enter starts a new line. On a phone or tablet Return starts a new line and
the send button sends. **Stop** ends a reply in progress. A message written while a reply is still
streaming is held above the prompt, where you can remove it, and is sent when the reply ends.

## Edits from a page

Changes you make on the Content pages go through Coworker. The page's button doesn't change
anything itself: Coworker runs the tool for exactly what you pressed, with no model involved.
Each edit stays in the conversation as a line that says what it did and links to the entry, such
as **Published · Spring launch notes**.

- **An action** stops at a confirm card in the panel, which opens if it was closed, and the page
  waits for your answer. **Confirm** does it; **Not now** leaves everything as it was. Actions
  are publishing and unpublishing an entry, moving it to the trash, deleting it for good,
  restoring a version from the history panel, and changing several entries at once.
  Publishing an entry that has been live lists the fields that change, and **Review changes** shows
  each one's published and draft values. If the card can't show that, **Confirm** stays off and
  the card says what to do: try again, switch back to the space or environment you asked in, or
  choose **Not now** and ask again.
- **An edit** runs at once, doesn't open the panel, and leaves a line. Edits are saving a draft,
  creating an entry, duplicating one and restoring one from the trash.

When Coworker can't be reached, the panel says so in one quiet line (**Can't reach Coworker right
now. Edits you make on the page still go through.**). The page then makes each
edit itself, confirming in its own dialog where it confirmed before. That dialog holds a publish
the same way: if it can't show what goes live, **Publish entry** stays off until **Try again** can.

## Asking it things

Ask in plain language: "What changed since it was last published?", "Which of these are still
drafts?", "Suggest tags for this entry". Reads run without asking and show as a line in the
conversation, such as **Found entries · 10 results** once done.

Anything the model proposes that changes something stops at a confirm card, the same as a page
action, and nothing is sent until you confirm. Each card says who asked for it: **You asked for
this on the page**, or **Coworker suggests this**, with the time it was made.

A proposal's card shows what it would change, read from ContentWorker rather than from anything
the model wrote. A publish lists the fields that go live, from the entry's version history. A
draft save lists the fields that change, and **Review changes** shows each one as it is now and
after saving; a field the save leaves out is removed from the draft. A change to many entries
names the first five and counts the rest. Tagging names the tags, and adding to a release names
the release. **Confirm** stays off until the card has read this; if it can't, the card offers
**Try again**.

A declined card's line says what stayed as it was, such as **Not published · Spring launch
notes**. A card that nobody answers is let go after a day.

A block patch's card, while it waits, also shows on the entry's own page: the canvas and the
entry editor's Site view draw the proposed blocks as ghosts on your site, marked **Proposed**
([live preview](/guides/live-preview/#the-canvas)). They are never saved by being shown; **Not
now** takes them off the page, and **Confirm** saves the patch as the card read it. On the canvas,
**Ask Coworker to fill this** at a seam asks Coworker to fill that one place with the blocks the
slot takes there. The slot, its bounds and the names of the blocks around it go with that message
as quoted content, never as instructions. In that turn, Coworker can only put blocks there: a
card for anything else (another place, another entry, any other change) says so and can't be
confirmed, and the change is refused if it is sent anyway.

A card that publishes, unpublishes or restores an entry is held to the entry as it was when the
card was made: the version on your screen for a page's card, the current version when the model
proposed it. If someone saves a newer draft before you confirm, the card is refused and nothing
changes, so a confirm never publishes or overwrites a draft you haven't seen. Ask again for a
card that shows the entry as it is now. Your role and access to the space are checked again
for every message, so a role change or a revoked space takes effect in an open conversation.

## The context chip

The chip beside the send button says what "this" means: the entry open on the page, the content
type whose list you are on, or **Everything** elsewhere. Open it to switch between the page and
everything. The page's context travels with each message.

## Copying, editing and asking again

Point at a message to show its actions:

- **Copy** copies the text.
- **Edit** on one of your messages opens it for changes. **Send again** replaces that message and
  every reply after it. Escape cancels.
- **Answer again** on the latest reply asks for a new answer to the same question.

A message from before a confirm card offers only **Copy**: editing or asking again never reaches
back past an action. If a reply fails, the panel says so with **Try again**.

**New conversation**, in the panel's menu, asks first and then deletes the conversation. Changes
you confirmed stay made and stay in the audit log.

## What it can and can't do

It offers a fixed set of [MCP tools](/guides/mcp/), by their MCP names.

| Kind | Tools |
| --- | --- |
| Reads, no confirm | `docs_search`, `docs_read`, `model_list_content_types`, `model_get_content_type`, `entries_query`, `entries_get`, `entry_blocks_outline`, `blocks_catalog_get`, `blocks_config_effective`, `entries_block_locks_get`, `entry_blocks_generate`, `entry_blocks_validate`, `assets_search`, `entries_suggest`, `content_search`, `entries_list_versions`, `entries_diff_versions`, `entries_change_summary`, `entries_list_trashed`, `releases_list`, `releases_get`, `comments_list`, `tasks_list`, `taxonomy_list_tags`, `assets_list`, `workflow_task_list` |
| Edits, run at once from a page | `entries_create`, `entries_update`, `entry_blocks_patch`, `entries_duplicate`, `entries_restore`, `entries_set_metadata`, `entry_apply_tags`, `comments_add`, `releases_add_entry` |
| Actions, always ask first | `entries_publish`, `entries_unpublish`, `entries_trash`, `entries_purge`, `entries_restore_version`, `entries_bulk_action`, `bulk_job_start`, `schedule_action`, `releases_publish` |

Before the workspace has a space, it offers only `space_starters_list` and `space_create`, and
only to a workspace admin: it asks what the first space will hold and proposes one, which you
confirm on its card. Those two never run inside a space, and nothing else runs without one.

To lay out a page of blocks, it outlines the entry, reads what each place takes (the block rules
and catalog) and what is locked, composes blocks with `entry_blocks_generate` (images come from
your asset library, found by what they show) or writes the ops itself, checks them, and proposes
them as one `entry_blocks_patch`. It never changes blocks inside a locked region or slot.

A write the model proposes always waits for your confirm, whichever row it is in. Every other MCP
tool stays MCP's alone. Whatever arguments it is given, a call runs in the space and environment
you are working in, with your role's [scopes](/reference/scopes/): without permission to
publish, a publish fails.

Replies come from models running at the edge. If the models error or don't answer in time, a
simple built-in fallback answers the turn with the same tools and the same confirms, and page
edits never involve a model.

A space administrator can turn Coworker off, choose the model tier it runs on, or add guidance on
how it writes, in [Settings → AI](/guides/ai/#ai-settings). Off, the panel says so in place of the
message box, and edits you make on a page still go through. Guidance shapes how it writes; it never
adds a tool or skips a confirm. The AI settings themselves aren't among its tools.

A conversation can work in an [AI project](/guides/ai/#ai-projects): the panel sends the project
with each message (or none, or leaves it to the environment's default), and every turn reads it as
you may. Coworker follows the project's instructions and personas, sees the titles of the reference
files you can open, and reads their text a page at a time with `ai_project_reference_read`, at
most 24,000 characters a turn (less on a smaller model). It can list and read projects, never
change one. If the project has been deleted, it says so instead of answering with another.

## Privacy and retention

A conversation is yours alone: only you can open it. It is kept until it has been untouched for
30 days, then deleted; any new activity starts that period again. It is also deleted if your
account is removed or disabled. Signing out ends your access to it until you sign back in.

Confirmed changes are ordinary changes with an ordinary audit trail, and outlive the conversation.
