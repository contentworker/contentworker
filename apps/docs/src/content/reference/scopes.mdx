## Narrowing further

- The [built-in roles](/guides/team-and-roles/) (Viewer, Approver, Editor, Manager, Administrator)
  are named scope sets, each including the one before it.
- A **role** adds per-content-type grants (`read`, `write`, `publish`) and per-field rules
  (denied or read-only) on top of scopes.
- A **personal access token** or an **OAuth token** never exceeds the person it acts for: its
  scopes are the intersection of what it was granted and what that person's role allows now.
- A workflow [stage](/guides/workflows/#stages) or [approval](/guides/approvals/#approvals) names
  the scope needed to move an entry into it or decide it.
